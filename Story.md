**Garden By The Shore
Prologue**
	As a female child, she was bullied and ostracized and even more after protecting a fox - her friend - from cruelty.
Seeking refuge by the sea, she was tragically swept away by the rising tide and drowned. Fortunately or unfortunately
for her, her skeleton crossed the path of a mermaid, a myth, or maybe more of a spirit ?

The mermaid pushed her remains onto a shore, the shore of a country called Japan. A land rich in spiritual energy,
where she was reborn as a hybrid spirit, mixed between what you’d call a mermaid and a fox-like entity. Upon regaining
her mind, she screamed a horrific cry, ripping her fish tail apart… Scale by scale…
